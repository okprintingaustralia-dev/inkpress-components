Every foil printing method has its strengths. Here's a simple comparison to help you choose the right option for your project.

Traditional Foil

Often chosen for larger production runs, where custom tooling becomes more cost-effective.

Requires a custom metal plate
Requires a separate cutting die
Ideal for larger order quantities
Higher setup costs
Offers embossed & debossed finishes

Digital Foil (Inkpress)

Often chosen for projects that value flexibility, smaller quantities and shorter production times.

Requires no custom metal plate
Requires no cutting die
Ideal for smaller order quantities
Lower setup costs
Offers a smooth, flat metallic finish
